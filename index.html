ABOUT THE PROJECT

Godrej Rivershore Plots

Rivershore is a premium plotted development spread across 75 acres near Samruddhi Mahamarg, offering over 1000 plots ranging from 1500 to 3000 sq. ft. Designed for both investment and lifestyle living, this project stands out with European-style architecture, a grand riverside clubhouse, scenic walking trails, and lush green surroundings. Backed by two successfully delivered projects, this third phase is bigger, more refined, and crafted for modern living.

Strategically located near key landmarks like MIHAN, AIIMS, IIM, IT hubs, DMart, and Samruddhi Circle, the project ensures excellent connectivity and strong future growth potential. With attractive pre-launch pricing starting from ₹83.99 Lakhs and limited-period discounts, Rivershore by Godrej Properties is rapidly emerging as one of Central India’s most preferred investment destinations, offering a perfect blend of infrastructure, nature, and long-term value.Surrounded by lush greenery, landscaped open spaces, and excellent connectivity to key hubs of Bengaluru, this development offers an elevated lifestyle for both end-users and long-term investors.
